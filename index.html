// MISSION STATEMENT

Cells are the fundamental units of life. Their precisely orchestrated processes of differentiation, development, and aging unfold into complex yet orderly life cycles, governed by intricate spatiotemporal regulatory principles, Cellular SpatioTemporal Dogma. Our group is dedicated to developing cutting-edge technologies for plant genomics—both "Reading" (e.g., single-cell and spatiotemporal multi-omics) and "Writing" (e.g., genome editing, synthesis, and automated systems). By integrating biotechnology (BT), information technology (IT), automation technology (AT), and artificial intelligence (AI), we now have an unprecedented opportunity to decode the cellular spatiotemporal dogma, bridge the knowledge gap between genotype and phenotype, and accelerate smart breeding for sustainable agriculture.

// ACADEMIC FOCUS

"Decoding the Cellular Spatiotemporal Dogma of Plant Development to Breed the Future"

Leveraging high-resolution multi-omics big data at single-cell and spatiotemporal scales bridges the genotype-phenotype knowledge gap and bypasses conventional agricultural bottlenecks.

Achievements

Consortium Leadership: Dr. XIA Keke acts as a core founding member of the STOC Plant (Plant Spatiotemporal Omics Consortium), aligning with scientists worldwide to construct a comprehensive multi-species single-cell spatiotemporal developmental blueprint. Under his leadership, his team has successfully developed plant single-cell and spatial transcriptomics technologies and generated single-cell spatiotemporal transcriptomic atlases of Arabidopsis leaves, callus formation, shoot regeneration, seed germination, and rice seed development. Recently, together with scientists worldwide, he has launched the big science project, Wheat SpatioTemporal Atlas project (Wheat STA), constructing a comprehensive spatiotemporal single-cell atlas of wheat to safeguard wheat smart breeding.

Smart Breeding Innovation: In the direction of genomic "Writing", he focuses on the research and development of gene editing, universal adapter, and cell fusion technologies to overcome the challenges of distant hybridization and grafting techniques. He is responsible for developing the gene editing technology for Phalaenopsis orchids and the creation of auto-luminescent tomatoes, tobacco, and orchids. He is currently developing an automated plant gene manipulation system and applications to achieve automated, intelligent, and digital operations such as tissue culture, genetic transformation, gene editing, screening, identification, and phenotypic sorting, which will significantly increase the efficiency of crop gene editing breeding, shorten the editing cycle, and save labor costs.

In recent years, he has published 15 papers in journals such as Cell, Nature Genetics, Developmental Cell and PNAS, and applied for 8 invention patents. His works were selected as one of the "Top Ten Advances in Chinese Life Sciences for 2022" and "Top 10 Science and Technology News in China for 2025".
